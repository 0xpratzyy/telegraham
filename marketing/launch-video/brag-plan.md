# Pidgy launch video: plan

**What it is:** A local-first Mac launcher that tells you who's waiting on you in Telegram and finds any message in two keystrokes.

**For:** People whose work lives in Telegram: BD, partnerships, founders, community operators.

**Angle:** The founder's own story from pidgy.chat. It opens on 1,571 contacts and a forty-screen inbox, where the reply that mattered was buried in a group called "stuff". Pidgy is the system you've been pretending you have.

**Hook (0–4s):** Black screen, then "1,571 contacts." and "A forty-screen inbox." as an endless chat list starts to scroll.

**Tone:** `polished` crossed with the site's golden-hour calm. Serif headlines, long holds, soft fades. The only motion is the camera drifting over the pidgy.chat illustration, plus the product being used.

**Visual identity:**
- The pidgy.chat illustration (sky, tree, kid napping), with the site's pigeons drawn as silhouettes.
- Site tokens (`#5B8DEF` accent, `#1D6FF3` CTA, orange ON ME badges).
- App tokens for product screens (`#2B2B2B` / `#323232` / `#393939`).
- Newsreader, Inter and JetBrains Mono.

**Sound:** The site's own `golden-hour-haze.mp3` (80 BPM, A major), looped once on a bar line. Soft effects sit under it in A-major pentatonic: key clicks, cursor clicks, pops, air, wings and chimes.

## Storyboard (60s, 20 bars of 3s)

| Time | Scene | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Inbox | "1,571 contacts." then "A forty-screen inbox." while the chat list starts scrolling |
| 6–12s | Buried | The list stops on the group "stuff" (Aman: "can you send the wallet?", ON ME). "The reply that mattered most was buried in a group called 'stuff.'" Then "The moment had passed.": the badge fades and 3d becomes 2w |
| 12–21s | Hero | Cut to the sky and tilt down to the tree while pigeons cross. App icon, "Pidgy", "Every message finds its way home." |
| 21–30s | Reply queue | The real dashboard. Home greeting with the pigeon flock, a cursor clicks Reply queue, then the top row, then the inspector opens (ON ME, suggested action, evidence). The camera pushes in. "What they're asking. What to say next." |
| 30–36s | Find | ⌘⇧T, then the real launcher. The query "where did I send the wallet to Aman" returns WALLET + YOU matches. "The message, not the chat." |
| 36–42s | Prep | The same launcher. "what did we decide with Akhil?" returns a ✦ PIDGY answer and FACTS. "One bounded summary. 10 seconds." |
| 42–48s | Values | "Pidgy notices. You decide." then "Everything stays on your Mac." |
| 48–54s | Channels | "One launcher. Every place work happens." Telegram LIVE; Slack, Gmail and WhatsApp SOON |
| 54–60s | End | "The system you've been pretending you have." Lockup, Request access, pidgy.chat. The camera settles on the kid under the tree |

Product screens are rebuilt from the SwiftUI source and the Aug 15 audit screenshots with demo data. The real screenshots contain personal contacts and email addresses, so they are not used directly.
